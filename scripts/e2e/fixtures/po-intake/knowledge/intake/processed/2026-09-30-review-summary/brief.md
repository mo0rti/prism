# PO Brief - Review summary export

## What is the feature?
A reviewer who has finished reviewing a document can export a one-page summary of their review: the document title, the reviewer, the decision (approved, changes requested or rejected) and the comments they left.

## Who needs it?
Document reviewers in our legal operations team.

## What problem does it solve?
Reviewers currently copy their comments into an email by hand after every review. It takes about ten minutes per document and comments get lost.

## Why does this matter to the business?
Retention: reviewers named the manual summary as their biggest frustration in the last two feedback rounds.

## What does "done" look like?
A reviewer opens a finished review, chooses export, and receives a summary they can send on without editing.

## What must NOT happen?
The summary must never include comments from other reviewers, and it must not be available while the review is still in progress.

## Does this feature need an advisory board review?
No

## Source
Legal operations feedback round, 2026-09-30.

## Raw notes
"I just want to press one button and have the summary ready to send." - reviewer, legal operations
