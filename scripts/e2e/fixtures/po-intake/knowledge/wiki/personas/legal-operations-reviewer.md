## Who they are
A member of the legal operations team who reviews documents and records a decision (approved, changes requested or rejected) along with comments.

## Goals
- Send on a summary of a finished review without editing it.
- Have every comment they left reach the recipient.

## Pain points
- After every review they copy their comments into an email by hand, which takes about ten minutes per document.
- Comments get lost in the manual copy.
- They named the manual summary as their biggest frustration in the last two feedback rounds.
